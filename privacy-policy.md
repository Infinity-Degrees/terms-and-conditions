## A. Scope & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Infinite Degrees, LLC ("Ecliptic," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our websites, mobile applications, and related services (the "Services"). Ecliptic is a social platform for action sports where users film tricks, get scored, compete, and interact.

---

## B. Personal Information We Collect

**Account & Profile:** Name or handle, email address, password, profile photo, bio, sport preferences, skill level.

**User Content:** Trick videos, photos, posts, comments, messages, event listings, and other content you create or share.

**Bisk Data:** Bisk balance, transaction history (purchases, transfers, withdrawals, earnings), and related records.

**Usage & Device:** App and web activity, log files, device identifiers, operating system, browser type, crash diagnostics, and performance data.

**Location:** GPS location data (if you grant permission) for event discovery and local content features. You may control location permissions in your device settings.

**Communications:** Messages sent through the Services, reports you submit, and support correspondence.

**Transactions:** Subscription tier, purchase history, payment tokens. Payment processing is handled by our payment providers (Stripe, Apple, Google). Ecliptic does not store full credit card numbers.

**Scoring & Competition Data:** Trick scores, rankings, leaderboard positions, competition participation, and related performance data generated by our automated scoring systems.

**Cookies & Similar Technologies:** Identifiers, analytics tags, and advertising tags as described in Section M.

---

## C. Sources of Information

We collect personal information:

- Directly from you (registration, content creation, purchases, support requests);
- Automatically from your device (usage data, device info, crash logs);
- From third-party services (single sign-on providers such as Apple and Google, analytics providers, payment processors); and
- From other users (tags, invites, reports, Bisk transfers).

---

## D. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to:

- Operate, secure, and improve the Services;
- Create and manage your account;
- Enable social features (profiles, following, messaging, content sharing);
- Process and display trick scores, rankings, and leaderboard positions;
- Facilitate Bisk transactions (purchases, transfers, withdrawals);
- Process subscriptions and in-app purchases;
- Provide customer support;
- Communicate about updates, security alerts, and policy changes;
- Moderate content and enforce our Terms of Service;
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized Bisk activity;
- Comply with legal obligations; and
- Personalize features and content recommendations.

## E. Sharing & Disclosures

We may share personal information with:

(a) **Service providers:** Hosting, analytics, payment processing (Stripe), moderation, customer support, and other vendors that help us operate the Services;

(b) **Other users:** Your public profile, posts, trick videos, scores, and other content you choose to share are visible to other users. Bisk transfers are visible to the sender and recipient;

(c) **Law enforcement and authorities:** Where required by law, legal process, or to protect the safety of users or the public;

(d) **Business transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of our assets; and

(e) **With your consent or direction.**

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. See Section J for information about targeted advertising.

## G. Children & Minors

The Services are intended for users age 13 and older. Users under 18 must have parental or guardian consent and supervision as described in our Terms of Service.

**COPPA compliance.** We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it and terminate the associated account promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact mia@idx.style.

**Minors age 13-17.** We collect only the personal information necessary to provide the Services. Parents or guardians may request to review, delete, or restrict the processing of their minor child's personal information by contacting mia@idx.style.

## H. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Specific retention considerations include:

- **Active accounts:** Data retained while your account is active;
- **Bisk records:** Transaction records retained for a minimum of 7 years for financial compliance;
- **Deleted accounts:** Personal data removed from active systems upon deletion, except where retention is required for legal, safety, fraud prevention, or financial record-keeping purposes;
- **Inactive accounts:** Bisk expires after 12 months of complete inactivity per our Terms of Service.
